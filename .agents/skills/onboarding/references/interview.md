# Interview

Ask round by round. Examples of options are hints, not a script: build
options that fit what the user already said.

## Round 1. Goal and audience

1. What should the site achieve? (sell a service, collect inquiries,
   inform, show a portfolio, a small tool)
2. Who visits it, and on what device, mostly?
3. What is the one action a visitor should take? (call, write, buy
   elsewhere, sign up) This is the main button.

## Round 2. Features and pages

1. Pages beyond the home page (O nas, Oferta, Cennik, Kontakt, one page per
   service, FAQ, blog).
2. Features (calculator, price table, gallery, map, blog, contact form).
   Mark what needs a server.
3. Languages: Polish only, or more?

## Round 3. Brand and domain

1. Brand name, and the company behind it (legal name, e-mail, phone,
   address, NIP for the footer; anything missing stays empty).
2. Domain: bought, planned, or not yet.
3. Logo and favicon files: yes (where) or not yet.
4. Google Tag Manager ID ("GTM-..."), or none for now.
5. GitHub: personal account or `Take-Group`?

## Round 4. Look and tone (DESIGN.md)

1. Mood in three words; propose 3 contrasting sets to pick from.
2. 2-3 sites they like, and what exactly.
3. Colors: from the logo, a color they want, or your proposal (propose 2-3
   palettes with hex and roles, none of them generic white-and-blue).
4. Fonts: propose 2-3 pairs (self-hosted, `@fontsource`).
5. Shape and density: sharp or rounded, airy or compact.
6. Images: photos, illustrations, or none; icon style.
7. Tone: "ty" or "Pan/Pani", casual or formal.
